# Plain prose

Read before you publish a page (SKILL.md step 5). A check cannot match these, so read for them and
rewrite each one.

Rewrite abstract metaphor nouns, feelings instead of facts, dense sentences, passive voice,
adverbs, fancy words, mannered prose and over-compression. Keep to at most 25 words in a sentence, 6 sentences in a
paragraph and three nouns in a row, and use one word for one thing (ASD-STE100). For the rest, Wikipedia's "Signs of AI
writing" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing).

Two page-specific rules:

- **Real names.** Use the name in the code: "the config loader" becomes `loadConfig`.
- **Test.** Could the sentence sit unchanged in another project's docs? Then give the measure
  ("Search takes 80 ms, not 900 ms").
